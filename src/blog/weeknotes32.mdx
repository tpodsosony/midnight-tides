- Got Arcs!!!!!!!!!! It's a very cool space war board game that I've been eyeing for ever. I've been asking to order it around forever at local stores but none of the ones I frequented had it in stock or were even able to order it. That may have come from the fact that the game switched publishers as the lead designer bought the rights to it and started his own company. BUT! There was one store in the city that had it, and I immidiately bought a copy as soon as I came back from toronto. The cover is so pretty and it sits proudly on my shelf when I'm not begging friends to play it with me :). 

<ul>
<li style="list-style-type:none;"> The game itself has just the right amount of comlexity for me. It simplifies the decision making process of war games by limiting your decisions to a trick taking game played to determine actions for every round. Each suit essnetially has different actions and to determine what you can do is based on factors such as being the leading player, having a higher card within the suit, or playing a different suit to limit your actions but to diversify your option to something outside of said leading suite. On top of that, how points are scored for a chapter can be determined by the leading player in a trick with the declaration of an ambition. Said ambitions differ and determine what players should be doing to maximize their score. There's so much depth to the game and I am so excited to explore more of it!!! (Please play with me people I need this game like a vampire needs blood).</li>
</ul>

### Other media 🎮📚🎬

- I finished Malazan: Book of the Fallen!!!! Easily my favourite fantasy series. Plenty of thoughts about the finale and series as a whole I am writing in a different post coming up. Series challenged the way I think about the world which I always love, and very rarely expect fantasy to do. Staring at injustices right in the eye is one of the bravest things we can do. 

- Watched Prime Time which I loved. Very very strong cinematography. The decision to have the entire film shot in that grainy 2000's television quality brought out a lot. Showcasing the dark side of trying to monetize justice and the kinds of people who do. I will think about that 2nd last scene for a long time. Robert Pattinson's perfect impression of Chris Hansen. Lance Oppenheimer is a director I will be keeping my eye on in the future following this. Super excited for his documentary with Nathan Fielder in a week :0

- Chronovisor hit just the right spot for me. Carrying its Umberto Eco inspiration on its heart, the movie focuses on the research more than anything else. What if the Chronovisor was real? What evidence would be needed? How would an academic go about finding this missing relic of vatican science? Combining a mix of real documentation and prop evidence on the matter, the movie captures that feeling of unrelenting desire to learn so effortlessly. It often felt like I was reading a book during the movie, a feeling that no other movie I've seen has even come close to evoking in me.

- History of Concrete was my introduction to John Wilson and I gotta say I was impressed! What starts of as a movie that promises to focus on concrete moves very quickly into a explorative territory that goes along to wherever Wilson's mind takes him. Reminded me a lot of Fielders work where the focus was instead on the absurdity of everyday life and the uniqueness each person brings to it. Incredibly fun time :)!
